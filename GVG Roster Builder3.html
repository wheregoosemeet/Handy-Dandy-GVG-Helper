📖 How the Roster Builder works

Loading sign-ups

  • Drag & drop a Raid-Helper sign-up JSON onto the drop zone, or click it to browse.
  • The tool reads each sign-up's name, role, spec, and any notes automatically.

Core players

  • Open ⚙ Core Parameters at the bottom to define your fixed roster — name, side, group, and position.
  • If a core player signed up, they are auto-placed in their slot.
  • If they did not sign up, their slot shows <fill core member> — drag anyone from the Bench to fill it.

Drag & drop

  • Drag any name from Sign-ups or Bench into a slot on the board.
  • Once placed, the player is crossed out in the Sign-ups list.
  • Drag a player from a slot back to the Bench area to un-place them.
  • Click ✕ on a chip to send them back to the bench.

Slot sharing (2-way split)

  • Drag a second person onto an already-occupied slot — it splits horizontally: [ Player A | Player B ].
  • By default: left player covers Games 1–2, right covers Games 3–4.
  • Click the purple games badge (e.g. 1 2) to cycle the split boundary.
  • A slot can only ever hold 2 players — adding a third is blocked.

Notes & warnings

  • A yellow ⚠ on a player means they left a note in the sign-up. Hover over it to read it.

Saving & restoring

  • Click 💾 Save state to download a .json snapshot of everything — placements, splits, core config.
  • Drop that file back onto the drop zone at any time to restore exactly where you left off.
  • Use 🗑 Clear all to wipe the board and start fresh (core config is preserved).
