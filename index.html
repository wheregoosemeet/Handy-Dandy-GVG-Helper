How this works

GvG starts at 35:00 and counts down.

Click Start the moment you see the in-game timer match 34:40.

If you miss that window, just adjust the Minutes / Seconds fields to match the in-game timer and start from there.

Once running, this will call out jungle spawn times as the match goes on.
